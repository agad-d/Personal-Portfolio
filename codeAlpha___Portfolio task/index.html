About

A bit about how I got here

I started with plain HTML & CSS pages, then went down the usual rabbit hole — Git, React, backend basics, and now design systems. Alongside that I run a small digital products shop, build websites for local NGOs and schools, and spend spare hours on physics olympiad prep and small electronics builds.

I like projects that force me to learn something uncomfortable — a new API, a rendering quirk, a deployment pipeline that doesn't want to cooperate. This portfolio is one of those: built to practice layout, motion, and responsive structure end to end.
